## 3. Detailed Feature Accuracy Reference (Supporting Educational Notes)

### A. Daily Free Transfers on Solana
- **Minimum App Versions:** TokenPocket Android `v2.9.0+` and iOS `v2.11.0+`.
- **Daily Limit & Eligibility:** **3 gas-free transfers per account per day** covering **all SPL token transfers (e.g., USDT, USDC)**, **excluding native `$SOL` transfers**.
- **User Flow:** Open TokenPocket → Solana wallet → select SPL token (e.g., USDT) → tap `[Send]` (bottom-left) → on the transfer screen, the default network fee asset is SOL (and users may also choose USDT or USDC — all display `Gas-Free` while the 3/day quota remains) → confirm the fee reads `Gas-Free` → tap `[Confirm]` to broadcast.
- **Technical Context:** Solana natively charges a small transaction fee (50% burned) plus Rent for account storage. TokenPocket's service covers eligible SPL transfers strictly within the daily 3/account quota (subject to official FAQ conditions).

### B. Pay Network Fees With Tokens & Additional Cost-Savers on TRON
- **Why TRON Transfers Fail at 0 TRX:** TRC20 token transfers consume both **Bandwidth** (transaction size) and **Energy** (smart contract execution). Without sufficient resources, the TRON network burns TRX — blocking users who only hold USDT or USDD.
- **Pay Fees Directly in USDT:** Open TRON wallet → select USDT → tap `Transfer` → enter recipient TRC20 address and amount → optionally claim a daily subsidy or enter a redemption code → select `USDT` as the fee payment asset → verify fee → tap `Confirm`.
- **Pay Fees Directly in USDD (`iOS & Android v2.8.0+`):** Open TRON wallet → select token (e.g., USDD) → tap `[Transfer]` → tap `[Pay With]` (default is TRX) → select `USDD` → review Bandwidth & Energy rental details (an optional 20 TRX buy option is also shown) → verify fee shows `USDD` → tap `[Confirm Payment]`.
- **TRON PSM Bonus:** Users can swap `USDT <-> USDD` 1:1 losslessly via the **TRON PSM (Peg Stability Module)** directly inside TokenPocket (`Swap`).
- **Additional TRON Cost-Saving Features:**
  1. **Energy Rental:** Rent Energy directly on the transfer page instead of burning TRX (actual savings vary depending on live TRON network resource prices and conditions).
  2. **Daily Subsidies:** Current daily allocation of **3,240 subsidized transfers/day** on TRON, first-come, first-served, claimable on the transfer screen (subject to in-wallet eligibility and remaining daily quota; eligible subsidized transfers show `0` fee).
  3. **Network Fee Redemption Codes / Transfer Fee Coupons:** Obtained through official TokenPocket campaigns and redeemed on the transfer page for partial or full fee discounts depending on code terms.
